## Law (plain)

If marine animals show **clear avoidance or stress indicators**, the platform **withdraws** or **powers down propulsion**. Data is secondary to welfare.

**Approach and presence** must aim to **reduce disturbance** relative to typical small-boat + thruster methods — not add a new harassment vector.

**Form teachers are not the observation subjects.** Multi-taxon extraction does not justify harmful approaches.

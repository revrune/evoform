## §1 · The job

Stay near marine life **longer and quieter** than a thruster box, on a **small boat-deployable** platform, so sensors (and careful partners) can observe without wrecking the scene.

---

## §2 · How we beat the market

| Priority | Win |
|----------|-----|
| **1 · Primary** | Lower disturbance and quieter self-noise **plus** more efficient motion than a typical small prop AUV on the same mission — achieved by **streamlined form**, **shark-skin-inspired riblets**, and **sparse or undulating thrust**, each used only where it earns its place under physics and durability |
| **2 · Secondary** | Longer loiter hours on one battery |
| **3 · Tertiary** | Easy deploy (1–2 people, small boat) |

Numbers get filled when we measure competitors and prototypes. Order of importance is locked.

## §3 · Envelope

| Axis | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Size** | ~0.5–1.2 m first build |
| **Depth** | 0–50 m first wet tests · design toward 0–200 m |
| **Speed** | Efficient slow survey and hover as primary mode · ability to sustain ~1–2 m/s for efficient transit / closing distance · not a high-speed racer |
| **Power** | Battery · multi-hour loiter |
| **Sensors v1** | Hydrophone + stable camera (steerable preferred) |
| **Future depth roadmap** | Subsequent platforms will prioritise **volume-following compressible architectures** and **flexible composites**, with **pressure-tolerant electronics potting**, to go beyond 200 m while keeping efficiency and low self-noise. The same path — streamlined form, shark-skin-inspired riblets, and sparse or undulating thrust — supports progressive gains in transit speed for closing distance with marine mammals |

## §5 · Year-1 user

## §6 · Build posture · **LOCKED**

Start with a **streamlined body**, **reliable buoyancy**, **simple fairings**, and **quiet propulsion** that can be built with current makerspace and lab methods. Add biomimetic surfaces, advanced propulsion, and volume-following or flexible structures as the platform improves. **Any textured or mosaic outer surface must be designed so it stays clean and functional between missions.**

Mission

Hang with the water column

Evo Marine is the first prototype path under EvoForm: a platform built to hang with the water column, not to race through it. Stable sensor geometry. Calm motion. Hours-class presence for careful observation. Sister role to CetaVox · better dwell platforms, richer honest data.

This is prototype language · open research · not a finished product claim.

Living · Dermochelys coriacea

Living teacher

Leatherback turtle

Evolution already solved migration-scale efficiency, pressure-tolerant volume, continuous skin language, ridge geometry, and hydrofoil-style propulsion at large size. We transfer those mechanisms into manufactured form. The closer the performance, the more the craft will naturally share planform with the animal. Resemblance follows performance.

Architecture direction

Hull · energy · actuation

Hull Leatherback proportions · planform and longitudinal ridge cues with real hydrodynamic basis
Volume Size chosen to hold energy, dual compute, buoyancy, insulation, and observation payload
Motion energy Intermittent thrust with long coast · turtle stroke–glide pattern (~30% effort / ~70% glide in lab biomechanics)
Actuation Hybrid · buoyancy for depth · trim mass for pitch · sparse thrust and/or few controlled foils
Skin language Continuous fairing · ridge geometry as function, not ornament

Control philosophy

Stability lives in software

The avionics pattern.

High-rate sensing and computer authority free the body to favor efficiency, compliance, and quiet. Lessons from fly-by-wire aircraft and from field hydrobatic AUVs (trim, buoyancy, model-based control) inform the loop.

Path

  1. Solid attitude and depth loops (PID-class) with good state estimation
  2. Upgrade to model-based control (LTV-MPC class) when coupling and underactuation need it · periodic linearization, short horizon, real-time solver on the vehicle

Compute split

A lean stability computer · separate from the observation payload computer.

Grounding in prior work

What we build on

  • Leatherback ridges improve lift-to-drag at low angle of attack in published glider CFD · we build on that result.
  • Turtle propulsion studies show thrust concentrated in a minority of the cycle, with mass and low drag carrying the glide.
  • Hydrobatic research AUVs already demonstrate computer-owned pitch, depth, and agile maneuvers using buoyancy, moving mass, and model predictive control in the field.

Our open lane is the combination: leatherback-scale observation hull · hybrid buoyancy and sparse thrust · avionics-style authority · quiet presence for sensing.

Trial one · success frame

What success looks like

Multi-hour smooth depth and attitude hold · calm transit with long coast phases · sensors usable throughout. Depth band and endurance numbers will lock with scale.

Still open

Collaborators welcome

  • Preferred length and displacement for prototype 1
  • Foil count and degrees of freedom (simpler 2-DOF vs richer 3-DOF shoulders)
  • Exact sensor set for the stability loop
  • Shallow-trial depth rating and hours target

Geometry stays flexible until scale and foil choice are set. When scale or foil DOF locks, we issue a thin update block only.

Hypothesis · Synthetic + Living lanes

Active stability underwater — the missing FCC

Founder note · Rua · avionics systems · F-16

The F-16 Fighting Falcon is purposefully unstable. It flies more like a dart than a bird or a glider. A human cannot hold a stable trajectory without the flight control computer (FCC). That is a feature: relaxed static stability buys agility; the computer buys survival.

Core claim (working)

EvoMarine’s first prototype should treat attitude and path control the way high-performance airframes treat flight control: a dedicated control computer that can hold quiet trajectories in conditions where the hull alone is not “naturally” stable — especially hover, slow station-keeping, and low-disturbance loiter near marine life.

Why this matters for Path A

  • Quiet wins over rigid stability. A thruster box can be “stable” by brute force and still wreck the acoustic scene.
  • Co-presence wants soft authority. Small, frequent, coordinated surface/thruster corrections — not continuous prop wash.
  • Hydrophone-first layout only works if the platform can hold still (or crawl) without fighting itself.
  • Form teachers still matter. Leatherback planform and ridge language reduce the control burden; the computer closes the loop. Neither replaces the other.

Transfer map (avionics → water)

Air · F-16

Unstable airframe · FCC multi-axis rates · fly-by-wire · pilot commands intent; computer holds envelope.

Water · Path A (hypothesis)

Soft-stable or under-damped hull at loiter Re · underwater control computer · sensor fusion (IMU, depth, optional DVL) · operator or autonomy commands intent; loop holds quiet hover and slow transit.

Label

This is a design hypothesis with a strong engineering pedigree in air, not a claim that commercial AUVs lack all control systems. The gap we care about is quiet, long-dwell, co-presence-grade control — sparse actuation, hydrophone-safe, abort-on-avoidance ready — not raw depth rating or survey speed.

What moves into the prototype first

Ordered against Path A win order: quieter + efficient · longer loiter · easy deploy.

  • 1 · Control computer path — multi-axis attitude/depth hold; sparse thruster authority; log for tank noise and power (experiment #1).
  • 2 · Continuous-skin leatherback form — ridge language optional; no open osteoderm gaps on v1.
  • 3 · Hydrophone-first isolation — layout and mounts before fancy surfaces.
  • 4 · Buoyancy-assisted hover — reduce thruster duty cycle so listening wins.
  • 5 · Abort-on-avoidance law — lower disturbance than current methods; leave the scene.
  • Later · regime-matched riblets · steerable camera · volume-following / osteoderm study.

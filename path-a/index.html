A quiet vehicle shaped by a deep ocean teacher.

Stay near marine life longer and quieter than a thruster box, on a small boat-deployable platform, so sensors can observe without wrecking the scene.

Win 01

Quieter + more efficient

Lower disturbance and lower cost of transport than a typical small prop AUV — through form, regime-matched surfaces where earned, and sparse thrust.

Win 02

Longer loiter

Multi-hour station-keeping on one pack so listening and observation can complete without thruster wash.

Win 03

Easy deploy

Boat-deployable by one or two people. Field-real scale, not crane theatre.

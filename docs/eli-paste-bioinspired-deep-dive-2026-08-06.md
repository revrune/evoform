## Path A job card (LOCKED · permanent · judge all concepts against this)

**Job:** Stay near marine life longer and quieter than a thruster box, **small boat-deployable**, so sensors observe without wrecking the scene.

**Win order (locked):**
1. **Primary** — Lower disturbance + quieter self-noise **plus** more efficient motion than a typical small prop AUV on the same mission — via **streamlined form**, **shark-skin-inspired riblets where earned**, and **sparse or undulating thrust** (each only where physics and durability justify)
2. **Secondary** — Longer loiter hours on one battery  
3. **Tertiary** — Easy deploy (1–2 people, small boat)

**Envelope (first builds):**
- Size ~0.5–1.2 m  
- Depth 0–50 m first wet tests · design toward 0–200 m  
- Speed: efficient slow survey / hover primary · ~1–2 m/s transit when needed · not a racer  
- Power: battery · multi-hour loiter  
- Sensors v1: hydrophone + stable camera (steerable preferred)

**Build posture:** Start streamlined body · reliable buoyancy · simple fairings · quiet prop with makerspace/lab methods. Add biomimetic surfaces, advanced propulsion, volume-following / flexible structures as the platform improves. Textured surfaces must stay **clean and functional between missions**.

**Later depth roadmap (not first hull):** volume-following compressible architectures · flexible composites · pressure-tolerant potting — beyond 200 m.

**Non-jobs:** outrun a dolphin · harass for data · shark-skin sticker product · free-lunch thrusters · “robot turtle / robot whale” cosplay as the product claim.

## Claim (working)

Path A should treat quiet loiter and slow station-keeping as a **closed-loop control problem**, not only a hull-stability problem. A dedicated control computer can hold attitude and path when the platform is soft-stable or under-damped at loiter Reynolds numbers — analogous to how the F-16 flight control computer holds an intentionally unstable airframe.

## Founder rationale (Rua)

- F-16 is purposefully unstable (dart-like, not glider-like).
- Human cannot maintain stable flight without FCC / fly-by-wire.
- Underwater co-presence platforms need a similar class of system: not necessarily “unstable by design,” but able to **maintain stability and quiet trajectory under conditions where passive hull stability is insufficient or costly** (noise, thruster duty cycle, disturbance to marine life).

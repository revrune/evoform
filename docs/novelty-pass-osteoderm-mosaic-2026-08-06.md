## 3. Related art map (what *does* exist)

### 3.1 Leatherback ridge transfer — **closest marine vehicle neighbor**

| Item | What it takes | What it does *not* take |
|------|---------------|-------------------------|
| **Hernández-Jaramillo & Vásquez (2023)** *Biomimetics* 8(1):80 · PMC9944121 | Leatherback-inspired **shell shape + longitudinal ridges**; CFD L/D improvement; conventional **internal pressure hulls** (Al AA6061-T6 bays) + oil VBS | Osteoderm mosaic, suture kinematics, volume-following envelope compliance |
| **Colombian Patent NC2019/000840** | Bioinspired shell (per paper § conclusions) | Same scope as paper — hydro shell, not mosaic joints |
| Bang et al. ridge hydro experiments | Science input for ridges | Not a product hull architecture |

**Implication:** Ridge dual-use is **not** EvoForm whitespace. Mosaic **kinematics** still appears open relative to this neighbor.

### 3.2 Morphing / flexible outer hulls — **volume change, different mechanism**

| Item | Mechanism | vs osteoderm teacher |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| Miller, Gandhi, Rufino — morphing hull UUV (Ocean Eng. 2014; AIAA morphing hull work) | Rigid inner pressure hull + **variable-diameter flexible outer** via morphing trusses | Continuous morph skin / truss actuation — **not** tile–suture mosaic; not leatherback osteoderm geometry |
| Soft biomimetic UUVs / modular magnetic soft robots | Continuous soft bodies | Softness related; architecture different |

**Implication:** Industry already thinks about **outer volume change**. EvoForm’s angle is **tiled suture-linked compliance** inspired by a Living teacher, not “we invented flexible hulls.”

### 3.3 Mosaic armor (land / body) — **tiles without marine volume-following**

| Item | Mechanism | vs osteoderm teacher |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| Dragon Skin–class / ceramic mosaic armor patents (e.g. disc/tile mosaics in polymer) | Overlapping or juxtaposed hard tiles for **ballistic** flex | Impact armor; not depth-adaptive submersible envelope |
| Adaptive armor arrays (e.g. US10670375 variable-angle tiles) | Actuated tile angle for threat defeat | Military land armor |

### 3.4 Diver plate suits — **rigid elements on flexible garment**

| Item | Mechanism | vs osteoderm teacher |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| **US4356569** Armored skin diving suit | Rigid shield elements on flexible garment (shark deterrent) | Personal suit; impact; not vehicle envelope volume-following under hydrostatic load with compressible internal volume |
| Atmospheric diving suits (rotary joints) | Pressure hull with articulated joints for **limbs** | True pressure vessels; human occupancy; different problem |

### 3.5 Turtle AUVs (propulsion / silhouette)

Flipper hydrofoils, rigid ABS “turtle” shells, TurtleCam trackers — already catalogued in research note. **None** implement suture mosaic volume-following.

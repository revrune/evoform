## Job

Quiet survey at **1–2 m/s**. Prove that **span makes lift**.

Packed already dropped the motion teacher. Long already is the AUV. Do not draw a fourth hull.

## Build

- Two working foils, one each side, on an existing Path A hull (Outline span or cownose span)
- Continuous skin
- Mic at the quiet end
- Buoyancy holds depth
- Rear prop is **catch-up only** · **off** for every measurement run
- Ridges are an **on/off coupon**, not the identity of the hull

---

## Do not lock

- Leatherback **30/70** is **green turtle** (van der Geest 2022/2023). Leatherbacks cruise continuously (Eckert 2002: 0.56–0.84 m/s). Hatchlings do not glide like greens (Davenport 1987).
- Dudley 2014 (*PLoS ONE* e110701, *Leatherbacks Swimming In Silico*) is **tethered**. Zero free-stream. Stroke force, not a self-propelled cruise map. Thrust on both half-strokes. Reverse thrust at reversal is added mass. Distal foil does the work. Neonate frenzy is not a cruise law. Adult Strouhal is still unpublished.
- Teacher (leatherback flipper vs cownose wing) stays unlocked until the tank speaks.

---

## Kinematics to try, not to costume

- Heave and pitch
- Strouhal near **0.25** (Clark and Smits; Fish)
- Frequency for speed · amplitude roughly held
- Thrust follows *fA* (Liu 2025 · verify at use)
- Optional mild chordwise lag (~0.4 wave), not a stingray ripple
- Feather through reversal · do not copy neonate beat rate as cruise

---

## Measure at 1–2 m/s · all four · same runs

| Metric | Question | Kill |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Hydrophone** | Can the mic hear the sea, or only the vehicle? | Motors, foil slap, or flow noise own the band we came for |
| **Hold** | Depth and attitude without the prop | Hold needs continuous rear thrust |
| **Cost of transport** | Energy per metre at survey speed · foils vs catch-up prop · at equal quiet | Foils lose quiet to buy COT, or cannot beat prop without becoming a thruster box |
| **Wake** | Ordered reverse-Kármán / ring wake | Wake splits, goes broadband, or looks like a prop |

**Also kills the study**

- Foils that are small planes or paint
- Prop on during the four metrics
- Ridges claimed without the coupon off
- A new mockup instead of tank numbers
- Locking 30/70 as leatherback
- A costume animal

**Pass for this card:** two foils · four numbers · 1–2 m/s · prop off · ridges optional. Multi-hour hold is later.
